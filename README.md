Things to Check Before You Order

Before buying Contact Lenses Online Pakistan, it helps to know your correct prescription, including base curve and diameter if you're an existing lens wearer, not just the power number. Checking the expiry date and batch details on the listing is also worth doing, since contact lenses have a shelf life once the seal is opened. For first-time wearers, it's best to get a proper eye check-up first, since contact lenses fit differently from person to person compared to glasses.

Caring for Your Lenses After Ordering

Once your Contact Lenses Online Pakistan order arrives, proper handling matters as much as the lenses themselves. Washing hands before insertion, using the recommended lens solution, and replacing lenses on schedule (daily, monthly, etc.) all help avoid eye irritation or infection. Skipping replacement schedules to "save" lenses is one of the most common mistakes that leads to discomfort later.
